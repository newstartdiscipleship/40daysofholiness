But what would it look like if God injected his holiness into the created realm and people, someone, became holy? Is human holiness even possible? Yes, it absolutely is. You see, God became man and in the person of Jesus Christ.

This is incredibly valuable for us because it allows us to define what perfect human holiness looks like. We'll never have infinite power or be uncreated. We will never have infinite presence and be everywhere. We'll never know everything, but God never called us to do that.

But he did call us to be holy. So what does it look like for a human to be holy? And the answer is it looks exactly like Jesus. Nothing more, nothing less.

Holiness is Christlikeness. If someone asks you during this 40 days of focus on holiness, "What are you seeking for? What are you looking for?" Here's the simple answer: I'm seeking to find out how much of Jesus God can produce in me during this life.
