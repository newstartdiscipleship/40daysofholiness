Teaching

Jesus Shows Us Human Holiness

Holiness is Christlikeness. God’s holiness can seem like unapproachable light—infinitely above creation and purer than we can imagine. Yet in Jesus Christ, God became human and showed us what holiness looks like within a genuinely human life.

We will never be uncreated, all-powerful, present everywhere, or all-knowing. God has not called us to possess those divine attributes. He has called us to be holy, and Jesus is the perfect expression of human holiness. Nothing more and nothing less defines the goal.

To pursue holiness is to ask how much of Jesus God can produce in our hearts and lives: in our minds, attitudes, desires, words, and actions. Maximum holiness means maximum Christlikeness.

What Christlikeness Does and Does Not Mean

Christian traditions and Bible teachers do not all define perfection in the same way. The familiar claim that “nobody is perfect” is partly true, depending on what perfection means.

This teaching does not promise a state in which we never make a mistake, stumble, misunderstand, become tired, feel regret, need to apologize, or have room to grow. It is not a perfection that makes further improvement impossible.

Instead, the aim is the fullest Christlikeness God can produce in a human life. This preserves both our creaturely limits and God’s serious call to follow Jesus.

Seek the Full Measure of Christlikeness

If we have sought something more than Christlikeness, we should adjust our expectations. If we have settled for something less than full Christlikeness, we should adjust them in the other direction. Jesus is the standard of human holiness. His call to “follow me” is a call to follow him into holiness.

Fix your mind and heart upon Jesus. Ask what he can do today—and throughout this journey—to reproduce his likeness in you. We are not chasing an abstract religious quality; we are pursuing the character of Christ.

Guided Prayer

Guided Prayer: Becoming Like Jesus

Father, we praise you for revealing holiness not only as unapproachable light, but in the person of your Son. We cannot possess infinite power or knowledge, yet Jesus shows that holiness and genuine humanity belong together.

Give us one deep desire: to become like Jesus. With the psalmist, may we say, “When I awake, I shall be satisfied with your likeness” (Psalm 17:15).

Turn our attention away from our lack of strength and toward Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith (Hebrews 12:1–2). As we behold him, change us into his image with ever-increasing glory (2 Corinthians 3:18).

Holy Spirit, use the true picture of Jesus to awaken and deepen our longing for holiness. Thank you, Jesus, for being our supreme example. Amen.
