Saved by Grace for Holiness

We are saved by grace for holiness. God does not wait for us to become holy before he loves and rescues us; he delivers us from slavery to sin and then leads us into the holy life for which he saved us.

A common lie says, “I must become holy and get my act together before God will want, love, and save me.” That reverses the gospel. Holiness is God’s purpose for us, but his gracious rescue comes first.

This is God's main point. It's what he's trying to produce in us. That's true. He is trying to create that in your life and your heart.

So what happened to that? Well, let me teach you something really, really valuable. You have to understand this. Salvation is by grace for holiness.

Grace Comes Before Holiness

God saved his people from slavery before they were holy in his sight. He delivered them from slavery because of his kindness to them, not because they had their act together. They were mixed up. They were stubborn in a million ways.

They were not holy, but he delivered them anyway, and we call that grace. Now, I want you to notice that God did not try to leave them in slavery and teach them holiness. Instead, he brought them out, and then he takes them to the mountain, the children of Israel. He brings them out of slavery in Egypt and takes them to Mount Sinai and teaches them holiness.

He makes his covenant. That's where he says, "I am the Lord your God. Be holy because I am." The exodus from Egypt came before the covenant of holiness.

Deliverance was all about grace, but it was for the purpose of holiness. Your salvation is not because you've been good or you've been holy. It was completely and totally of grace. No contribution of goodness on your part at all.

God did it because of past promises he made to display his glory as a saving, faithful God. He made promises to Abraham, and he's keeping those. He made promises to Jesus, and he's keeping those. Your salvation was by grace, but your salvation is for the purpose of holiness.

So since I don't know everybody that's watching this and going through this 40-day journey with me, I have to make sure you understand. Holiness isn't so God will love you. It's not so that God will save you. The covenant of holiness God makes between him and his people, and that's you and me, is coming after he delivers us from bondage and slavery to sin.

Saved by Grace, Created for Good Works

So the first thing that I have to ask you is this. Have you been saved? Have you trusted in Jesus and been saved from the penalty of sin by faith in his blood? Now, if you've been thinking, "Well, I'm going to get holy and then God's going to be pleased and save me," then you've got it exactly backwards.

You're saved by grace for holiness. God wants to save you not because of any holiness or goodness that you've gotten a hold of, but because of his kindness and his grace showered on us through Jesus Christ. Ephesians 2:8-9 says this, "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not of yourselves, it is the gift of God—not of works so that no one can boast." Now, if you're saved, you're not wrong to pursue holiness.

In fact, it's the most natural thing in the world to chase after if God's really saved you. The next verse after the one I just read, Ephesians 2:10, says, "For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do." You see the progression? You're saved by grace for good works, by grace for holiness.

Holiness Is Part of the Gospel

Now, don't miss this. Holiness is part of the gospel. It is the result of, it is the natural progression of the gospel. Some people treat salvation by faith like that's the gospel.

It's the good news, and holiness is like it's the irrelevant news. Don't do that. Your salvation, the whole point of it is for holiness. It's not just some eternal fire insurance where down the road you can be with Jesus when you die.

It's for the purpose of holiness now. God takes his people out of Egypt, then takes them to Sinai to make his covenant of holiness with them and to teach them about him. So it's the most natural thing in the world once you're saved to start pursuing holiness because that's literally what God saved you for. So if you're just realizing that you're not saved, no problem.

Let's get that set straight right now, immediately. Trust in Jesus today. Repent of sin. Leave your life of slavery to sin and death.

Do it right now because he's good and because he's kind, not because you're holy and you've got it all together. Jesus is your Moses. Let him lead you out of the land of slavery and bondage and lead you to his kingdom. He's here to do it now, not wait until you've got your act together.

But if you are already saved and you know you are, you know you're right with God, then realize this. God didn't save you for heaven or some eternal fire insurance or because he felt sorry for you. He saved you for holiness. That's what's next.

Don't forget. Don't skip over it. Take some time to think through, fill in blanks, think through the meditation questions because God's got work he's doing in you through the Holy Spirit. And so let me just encourage you to keep on going.
