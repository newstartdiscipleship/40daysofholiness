A Further Cleansing After Salvation

Cleansing after salvation addresses corruption within the believer’s heart, not merely the guilt of past sinful acts.

Remember, we're doing these 10 days, we are collecting metaphors for holiness. Metaphors are word pictures that teach us what holiness is, that spark our imaginations about it and inspire us to long for it. Jesus doesn't usually teach us in formulas or checklists or bullet points.

Usually, the scripture gives a word picture to capture and inspire our imagination and our longing. that's what this 10 days is about. today's metaphor is that holiness is a further cleaning, a further cleansing beyond salvation.
