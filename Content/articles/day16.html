Holiness Means an Undivided Heart

Holiness means an undivided heart—a heart united in reverence, love, and loyalty to God rather than pulled in competing directions.

Today we are in Psalm 86:11. we're reading David's words in Psalm 86:11, where he says, "Teach me your way, Lord, that I may rely on your faithfulness.
