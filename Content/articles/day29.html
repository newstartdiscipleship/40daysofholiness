Surrendering the Good and Holy to God

Surrendering to God includes not only abandoning evil but placing every good gift, ability, relationship, and holy desire in his hands.

But here's a distinction that I've found that might help you. Some people wonder what's different about surrendering now for the purpose of holiness versus surrendering for salvation. Well, in salvation, you're surrendering conflict, right? We're no longer at war.

We're no longer in active rebellion against God. But when we talk about surrendering to God as a Christian, what are we talking about there? Because we're not in rebellion against God anymore. What's left to surrender?

Well, if you've been following along, you probably have some idea of the answer and where we're going. But here's the way I would explain it. In salvation, we surrender the bad. In holiness, we surrender the good.

In salvation, we surrender rebellion. In holiness, we surrender our powers. "All my being's ransomed powers," somebody said. In salvation, we surrender evil, but in holiness, we surrender blessings.

When we surrender these blessings, we begin to see the problem more clearly. The problem is self. There's a story about Jacob in the Bible, in the Old Testament. Esau is coming, right?

And Jacob's always been this self-sufficient man. He can figure it out, and now he's up against a problem he can't solve. Esau's coming, and he sends ahead flocks and herds and servants, and then he sends Leah and her kids that were not so precious to him, and then he sends Rachel and her kids that are more precious to him, and then he stays behind on the other side of the river and prays. And he has to wrestle with God and wrestle with who he is as Jacob.

Place Every Good Gift in God’s Hands

He has to name the self. He has to name the deceiver. By the way, that's what Jacob means, the one who trips others, the one who grasps the heel. And then God blesses him.

In the same way, God may have you surrender many things that are in themselves good, like Jacob's flocks and herds and servants and family. He wants to get down to the point where he can have you recognize that the problem isn't other things. He wants you to see self clearly and surrender that. Self-will, self-sovereignty.

And then he will cleanse that self and make it wholly his and give you self-control as the fruit of the Spirit in its place. that's what we surrender. We surrender to God all that is good so that he can make it truly a blessing by dealing with the sin nature, the self-life that is behind it all. And that surrender is what makes the difference in our lives, in our lives of holiness and purity before God.
