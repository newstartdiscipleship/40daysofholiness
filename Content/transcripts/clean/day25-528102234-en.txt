Let's pray today over day 25, entire sanctification. Father, we thank you for the message of 1 Thessalonians. We thank you that you have changed us forever, that we are definitely safe. We thank you for the assurance that our life has been transformed by the grace of God. We pray, Lord, that you would give us an awareness, and you have given us an awareness of the urgency of what is needed and what is lacking still within us. We want, Lord, that you would give us such an awareness and such a hunger to know the fullness of the presence of the Holy Spirit, the powerful, full cleansing, and full sanctification that you have created for us and you have provided for us in Christ. So we pray, Lord, that you would sanctify us through and through, that as the God of peace, you would bring us not to a place of war with ourself, but that you would give us a place of peace, not only peace with you, but peace within us as well. That we might not be subtly under-the-radar sabotaging by our own selfishness, but would you bring us to a place of peace like you are a God of peace. And Lord, we pray for blamelessness. We pray that you would produce this within us, this entire sanctification, this through-and-through sanctification. Would you bring it to us, Lord, and bring it to us in such power that it gives us a blameless and pure life all the way till the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. We pray that you would do that in Christ's name, for His sake. Let's continue seeking the Lord together.
