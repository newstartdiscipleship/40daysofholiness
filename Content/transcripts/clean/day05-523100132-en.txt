Let's seek the Lord together on day number five. Father, we praise you today for revealing holiness to us, not just as unapproachable light where we would be convinced that we could never be holy. We can never be a spirit like you with all power and all knowledge. We can never be holy in human form, and yet you have showed us and revealed to us that humanity and holiness can coexist in the name and in the person of your son, Jesus. So Lord, we have one deep, supreme desire that we might be like Jesus. I praise you, Lord, for my prayer partner who is here praying with me today. I praise you for the desire that you've put within us to be like Jesus. Like David said, "When I awake, I will be satisfied with your likeness." Oh God, we want to be like Christ. We want to keep our mind fixed and stayed upon him, not focusing on ourselves or our own lack of strength, our own lack of ability. Lord, we've looked for too long at ourselves and never seen anything good happen by just looking at ourselves. But we look to Jesus today as the source and the definition of holiness. Oh God, help us to look up to him and be changed into his image with ever-increasing glory. Thank you, Father, for showing us what human holiness really does look like, and we rest upon that. We trust in it today. We ask that the Holy Spirit would use the image and the picture of Jesus to inspire us and help us long for holiness today. Thank you, Jesus, for being our supreme example. Let's continue to pray together and ask the Lord to bless and work in our hearts as we pray.
