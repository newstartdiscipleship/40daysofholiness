Let's pray together over day 21. Father, thank you for the assurance that all things are right between us and you. Thank you, Lord, for the confidence that we are given to Jesus by God the Father, that we have obeyed the word of God, and that we are not part of the world. We rejoice in that. We thank you for it. We praise you that we have been cleansed. We have been made clean by your word, by accepting it. Thank you, Lord. Now, you bring us to an awareness in this 40 days, you bring us to an awareness of deeper cleaning. We don't want, Lord, to trust only in what you have done for us in the past and to overlook things that you still wish to do, that you still have for us. When you have more for us, we don't want to just leave it on the table. So God, we pray that you would clean not only our consciences from dead works, but that you would clean us in a deeper, more fundamental way. We pray that your cleaning, your cleansing would go all the way down to our hearts, to our motivations, to our thought processes, to our mindset. We ask, Lord, that you would, like Peter talked about what happened in Acts 2, we pray, O God, that you would clean us and purify our hearts by faith. Jesus prayed that we would, who have been cleaned, would be sanctified. And we pray that you would do this for us, Lord, that you would sanctify those who have already been sanctified. We pray that you would sanctify us in a deeper and more fundamental and more powerful way. Lord, would you draw to us, draw us to Christ, and help us to rejoice in his purifying, sanctifying work for us on the cross, and let all of the results of that sacrifice be accomplished in our hearts and in our spirits, we pray in Jesus' name. Let's continue seeking the Lord together.
