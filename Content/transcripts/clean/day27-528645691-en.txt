Let's pray together over day 27, Holiness is something to be followed from Hebrews 12:14. Father, we thank you today for the privilege that it is to be called to holiness, called to be one with your essential nature, the core of your being. You have not chosen to give to us your presence everywhere or your infinite knowledge, but you have chosen to give to us the holiness that we need, and we wish to be there in your presence to see the Lord someday. So we pray for the grace to follow that holiness and to pursue after it. Thank you, Lord, that you have given us a relationship with you that grows and increases. So Lord, we choose to follow today. We choose to make progress. We choose to increase our desire for holiness. And Lord, we pray that you would help us as we've been learning over the last few days what it means to be holy and what holiness in the scripture means, even for those who have already been saved. And we pray that you would increase our desire for all that holiness means, for all that holiness is. Lord, give us a deep and passionate and lasting desire for the things of God to reproduce your character inside us. Lord, cleanse from our heart, put to death within us anything that is not like Jesus, anything that is not like you. Lord, if it costs us friends, if it costs us comfort or ease, or if it costs us our will and our way, we pray that you would take those things. They are nothing. They are worth nothing compared to the joy of knowing your holiness. Lord, we want to die to self and die to idols in such a way that we are able to live in full harmony with and follow deeper and deeper the holiness of God until someday we follow it right through the gates of heaven. We pray that you would give us a passionate commitment to this path of holy love and holy joys in Jesus' name. Let's continue seeking the Lord and praying together.
