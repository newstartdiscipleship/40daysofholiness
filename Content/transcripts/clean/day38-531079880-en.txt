Let's pray together over day 38, Holiness in the Emotions. Father, thank you for the assurance that your Holy Spirit calls us to a holiness that is complete, not just in our spirit or in our will, not just in our body, not just in our mind, but that your holiness has called to us to go all the way down to the core of our being, to where we really live. And we thank you for the opportunity to be holy in our emotions. Lord, I would pray that you would help us today, that as we give to you our emotions, as we place them before you in full surrender, that you would take those emotions and that you would revamp them. Would you use, Lord, the gift of imagination, and instead of letting it lead us toward corruption and lust and fear and anxiety, instead, Lord, I pray that you would help us to be able to create joy in you, create joy in who you are and in the glory of your character. Lord, I pray that you would give us the grace to cherish you and to rejoice in you and to see you in all the beauty of your character, all the glories of your attributes. Lord, you are all of the things that we need. You are the water of life. You are the bread. You are the treasure in the field. You are the pearl of great price. You are the strong tower. You are the wind that blows through our lives and gives us the freshness. You are our Lord of Hosts, the Lord of angel armies. You are all of these things, and we pray for the grace to feel as though it were true, to see it with clarity in our emotions, in our imaginations, so that what we know to be true in our minds would soak down and touch our emotions. We pray, O Lord, that you'd let yourself be very real to us today, not just in our hearts or not just in our minds, but in our emotions as well. In Jesus' name. Let's continue praying together as we seek the Lord for holiness in our emotions.
