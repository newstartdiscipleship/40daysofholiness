Let's pray together for day 22. Today's prayer is a beautiful hymn called "Love Through Me, Love of God" by the great missionary Amy Carmichael. "Love through me, love of God. There is no love in me. O fire of love, light thou the love that burns perpetually. Flow through me, peace of God. Calm river, flow until no wind can blow, no current stir, a ripple of self-will. Shine through me, joy of God. Make me like thy clear air, that thou dost pour thy colors through as though it were not there. O blessed love of God, that all may taste and see how good thou art, once more I pray, love through me, even me." Father, we do ask for the powerful work of God's purifying love in our hearts. We pray that we would be filled with the Holy Spirit. We pray that you would take full control of areas of our heart and life that we never realized before we had kept for self. We pray, Lord, that you would affect deeply and keep full continually all of the parts of our heart, our relationships, our speech, our music, our attitudes. Would you keep all of them full continually of the presence and power and purifying work of the Holy Spirit? And Holy Spirit, we invite you to do that work today. If there's any reason why you could not fill us today, now, and fully control every part of our life, reveal it to us so that we might turn our back upon it, repudiate it, and find the fullness that comes from you. Thank you for giving us yourself as a gift at the moment of salvation. Now fill us, we pray. In Jesus' name. Let's continue praying and seeking the Lord together.
