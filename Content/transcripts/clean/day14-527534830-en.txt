Let's pray together for day 14. Father, we are deeply thankful that you have given to us the holiness without which no one will see the Lord. You have gifted that holiness to us, not just legal forgiveness of sins, but also spiritually pouring into us the new life, breathing into us again the breath of life spiritually as you did for Adam physically at the beginning. We thank you, Lord. In a moment of time, we have been gifted holiness. We couldn't have earned it. We couldn't have worked hard enough to deserve it, but you have given us the gift of being one of your saints, one of your holy ones. Lord, thank you. Help us to live that out. You have declared that we are this, and now, Lord, help us to live out that holiness, not to leave holiness and being a saint to some imaginary group of super Christians. But Lord, make me a saint. Make me everything that you've claimed that I am. Everything that you call me, now produce it in me. Lord, I pray that you would help me and my friend who is seeking the Lord together with me here. Lord, would you help us to truly rise up and become the people that you say we are? We know that, Lord, that the holiness that you have given to us needs to be followed. It has a will. It has a plan. It has a direction. It has a goal for us. And so, Lord, we yield ourselves. We yield ourselves to the goal of holiness, to the goal of seeking after you. Oh, Lord, make over my character. Make over my heart until not only outwardly but inwardly I am everything that Jesus died to make me. We pray these things in His great and good name. Let's continue seeking the Lord together for the rest of this time in your own words.
