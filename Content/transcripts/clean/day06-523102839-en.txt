Let's pray together over the things that we've learned today. Father, you are a gracious, saving king. Your reign is holy and righteous, and I praise you for the deliverance that you've given us from bondage to sin and death. Oh, God, thank you for the gracious gift of eternal life through Jesus, your Son. Father, I praise you that you didn't leave it to ourselves to struggle along and lift ourselves up and save us, but in a moment of time, by faith in his blood, you have delivered us from the slavery of sin and bondage of our taskmaster, Satan, in all of his cruelty and the cruelty of his kingdom. He kept us locked under lock and key, bondage to sin, carnal, sold under sin, and yet you have delivered us from slavery, and you have set us free and adopted us into your family and justified us by faith in Jesus, and we thank you for the pure forgiveness and the cleansing of our conscience that you have given to us. Oh, God, thank you that you didn't bring this about through our own efforts or our own goodness, but you have brought us out of slavery, out of the land of Egypt, for the purpose of making us holy. Now, Lord, because of this great salvation that you have given to us, because of this great deliverance, we are asking, Lord, that you would take us like you did your people, Israel. Take us to the mountain and let us know what it means to be holy like you are holy. Lord, take us to that place where we see the fire and glory of your holiness, and we tremble before you, and we cry out, "Oh, you are holy. We wish to be like you." We want to be your people. We want to be holy like Jesus, holy like God our Father is holy. So Lord, teach us over the rest of this 40 days. Teach us the purpose for which you have called us out of bondage, we pray in Jesus' great name. Let's continue seeking the Lord together in your own words.
