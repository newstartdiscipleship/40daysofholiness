Let's seek the Lord together for the holiness that only comes from God. Father, you are amazingly, beautifully, eternally holy. I praise you, Lord, that you are the one that all holiness starts with and originates from. I praise you that just like your holiness communicated from you to the holy ground that Moses stood on and encountered you on, I want to encounter you. We want to see you in your holiness and the purity of it. We want to see your holiness and the beauty and the power and the glory of it. I praise you, Lord, that everything you touch, everything you possess is holy, just as you are holy. So, oh God, would you flow to me, flow through me, Lord. Let the holiness of God produce in me greater and greater holiness until everything about me is like everything about you. I pray, oh God, that you would fill new parts of my mind and my heart and my attitudes. I pray that you would fill places that I don't even know exist right now, that you would touch them and make them holy like you are holy. Make it, Lord, to where we shine. I pray for the person, Lord, the person who's reading this and listening to this, that's praying with me right now. I pray that you would fill them so full of your holiness that like the bush was burned and yet not consumed, they would flame with your holiness and yet not be burned and consumed, but be aglow with the beauty of your holiness. I pray this in Jesus' name. Amen. I would encourage you to continue to pray now and make your own appeal to the Lord for the beauty of his holiness to flow into you in the areas of your life that you need most. Let's continue in prayer.
