Let's pray together over day 15. Father, I want to thank you for the holiness that you've gifted to us in Jesus. Thank you, Lord, for the will that you have for our lives. You will that we should be holy. You want and desire and plan for our holiness, and we thank you for it. We prepare ourselves, Lord, like Peter commanded us to do, by getting our minds ready for action, and we want eyes to see. Like David prayed so many centuries ago, open our eyes that we may see wonderful things in your law. Lord, we pray for the right mindset, the right set of glasses on our eyes. Lord, please deliver us from interpreting the possibilities of grace through our own weakness. We're sorry, Lord, for bringing the possibilities, bringing the standard to which you've called us down to our own level and limiting God by my own weakness and my own sin and my own failures. Instead, Lord, would you help us to interpret our weakness through the possibilities of your grace? Would you help us, Lord, to have a heart that rejoices in possibility when we hear you call us to holiness? Lord, we choose to see our own weakness and our own inability and even our own sin through the lens of your great infinite grace, and we ask, Lord, that you fulfill all your promises in our souls. Lord, we praise you for the possibilities of grace. We repent for trusting our weakness more than your strength, and we ask that you would raise our eyes and help us to catch sight of the possibilities of grace. In Jesus' name. Let's continue praying and seeking the Lord together.
