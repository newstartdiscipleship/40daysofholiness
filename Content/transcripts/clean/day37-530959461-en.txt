Let's pray together over day 37, Holiness and the Emotions. Father, thank you for the gift of emotions. We are truly thankful. They sometimes are frustrating, but they're also wonderful. And so we pray that you would accept our gratitude for making us feel. You feel, and you made us like you, and we thank you for that. I praise you for revealing yourself as a God who is truly happy and joyful. We pray, Lord, that you would help us to find our happiness in our holiness, like you are happy in your holiness. We pray, Lord, that you would help us not to be a victim of our emotions, that they would not just happen to us, but that they would happen through us. We pray, Lord, that you would help us to, like the songwriter said, not trust the sweetest frame, but wholly lean on Jesus' name. Lord, don't let us lean into and depend upon our emotions to carry us along the way, but help us to rest in Jesus. Would you give us, Lord, the ability to fully trust in you and to have the joy of the Lord that results from a right spiritual focus in our minds and our wills. Would you help us, O God? Give us the grace to do that. Sanctify our emotions. Lord, sometimes we have been led around by the nose by our emotional state. We've gotten up in the morning and felt great and assumed that everything was great with you, even if it wasn't. Or sometimes we've gotten up and felt terrible or discouraged, and we've assumed that you had forsaken us or that we weren't right with you, even when we were. So, Lord, instead, set us free from depending on and being led around by our emotional state, and make us instead confident like Jesus is confident, resting in you every single moment of every day. We pray in Christ's name. Let's continue to pray and seek the Lord together.
