Let's pray together over day 33, Roots and Fruits in Our Lives. Father, thank you for the joy that it is to have a heart that is clean. Thank you, Lord, for the assurance that you can take and bend our life and bend our heart back straight to where we're not self-sovereign anymore. We allow you to rule and reign as you always are worthy of in our lives. Lord, we want to live not just free of self-sovereign nature of sin in a bent toward our own will and our own way, but we want to live so that we are growing past any areas of self-centeredness, any unconscious ways of thinking, cycles of automatic habitual behavior. Lord, would you help us today to not get careless, to not allow old ways of thinking, old patterns to just hang around in our life, but instead, oh God, would you sensitize us to the presence of something that can lead to a life that goes back to the way it used to be. Lord, would you help us to be so sensitive, so ready to hear that like Charles Wesley wrote, we would have a sensitivity to sin and a pain to feel it near. Lord, would you give us a heart that is confident in what you have done but is passionate about growing what you have done as well. Lord, help us to reprogram our minds, to reprogram our hearts. We don't want old patterns to hang around and grow bitter roots that bear bitter fruits. So instead, Lord, help us to be able to ask, is it a root or is it a fruit? And if it's a fruit, Lord, help us to have a heart of repentance, just to throw it aside and to expect that you will give us exactly what we need to grow in grace and holiness all the days of our life. In Jesus' name, we ask these things. Let's continue to pray together.
