Let's pray together over day 31. Holiness is beholding God's glory. Father, we thank you for the glory that you have displayed to us. You are incredibly, beautifully, wonderfully glorious, and we praise you for the beautiful outshining of your unique holy excellence. We praise you that it is jaw-dropping, earth-shattering, and soul-satisfying. We thank you, Lord, for the assurance that your glory is why you do what you do, and we behold that glory. We look to you today because the glories of Earth are fading and not lasting, and they are not satisfying. And so we turn our eyes to you, and we fix our eyes on Jesus, on the ministry of Jesus and the work of Jesus and the person of Jesus and the excellence of Jesus. We turn our back on the old way of a fading glory and a hiding from God and a bondage and a despair. And we turn our back on the old covenant that revealed holiness and could command holiness but could not empower holiness. Oh, thank you, Lord, for the new covenant and the power of Jesus through his blood and through the Holy Spirit that he's given. He has opened the door for us, and we look to his face. We look to his face and his character and his beauty and his finished work for us, and we fix our eyes on him. And we ask, oh Lord, that the glow of the glory of Jesus would soak down into us and that we would be transformed. Oh God, to be like what we see when we look at Jesus. Oh, to be like him, as pure as he is. So we pray, oh Lord, that you'd come in your fullness and in your sweetness and you'd stamp your own image deep on our heart as we behold your glory with an unveiled face. Thank you, Jesus. We encourage you to continue to pray and ask the Lord to do these things and accomplish these things in your heart.
