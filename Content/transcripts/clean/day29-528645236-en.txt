Let's pray together over day 29, surrendering the good and the holy. Father, we thank you today for the assurance that you have made us holy and right in your eyes at the moment of salvation. We thank you that we are adopted into your family, that we are fully pleasing to you in a moment of time, that we are acceptable in your sight and made acceptable by the blood of Jesus Christ through faith in his atoning sacrifice. We thank you, Lord, for the assurance that our hearts and our hands are being made clean by the blood of Christ, and that we are living in victory over sin, and that we are walking forward with you. And Lord, we take these bodies made holy, and these spirits made holy, and these talents made holy, and we yield them to you. We surrender to you. Lord, thank you for accepting our surrender of our rebellion against you at first. When we came to you in salvation, thank you for accepting the surrender that we offered you. And I ask, Lord, that you would help us to have the courage to surrender like Jacob and send away our blessings. Lord, as we do that right now, we surrender the blessings of life and hands and feet and mind and talents and voice and influence and family and relationships and love and blessings like finances and house and all these things that are good and holy. We have yielded them to you. There's nothing wrong with them. But oh, Lord, we don't wish them to be tainted by self. We don't wish them to be colored by self-will or affected by a desire for our own glory. So we surrender the good to you right now, Lord. We do this in Jesus' name. I'd encourage you to continue surrendering things. Whatever the Lord brings to your mind and your heart, those things that are good and blessing in your life, surrender them right now.
