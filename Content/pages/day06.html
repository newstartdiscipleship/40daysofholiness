Day 6 - God saves His People for Holiness.

Welcome to the 40 Days of Holiness discipleship journey! Today we're answering the question, "Why does a holy God save people?" God saves us to make us holy, to make us partakers of his own divine nature. We're made in the image of God, and by making us holy, God is restoring the image of God in us! 

Click the video above and learn about God saving his people FROM sin, FOR holiness.

Guided Prayers for Holiness experience: Your 40 Days of Holiness workbook contains a written prayer, and the 5-minute video prayer time above is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Heavenly Father, you are a saving, gracious God. Thank you for your amazing deliverance from my bondage to sin and death. Thank you for the gift of eternal life through Jesus your Son. Thank you that it did not come through my efforts or goodness. I know you have delivered me for the purpose of making me holy like You. Now, because of what you have done, take me to the mountain of your holiness, and teach me there, what it means to be like you.  Amen.
