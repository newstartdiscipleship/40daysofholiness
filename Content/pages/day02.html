Day 2 - All Holiness comes from God

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness, Day 2! God's holiness is his excellent, utter uniqueness and purity. But what about holiness in the world? Today, we talk about where holiness in people, places, and things comes from. Hint: All holiness comes from God, who is the source and definition of holiness! 

Watch the video for teaching, fill in the blanks in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, then take time to do the meditation questions on holiness. God's sanctifying work in you is worth the time!

Guided Prayer for Holiness: Day 2

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Father in Heaven, you are holy always and forever. Holiness flows from you. I praise you for making all that you possess holy, just like you are holy. I ask that you flow to me and through me, and that you produce in me in this 40 days, greater and greater holiness in every part of me. I want you to own new parts of me, fill new parts of my mind, heart, and attitudes, that I am not even aware of at this moment. I invite you to show me new places in my heart for you to fill and make them shine with your holiness. Amen.
