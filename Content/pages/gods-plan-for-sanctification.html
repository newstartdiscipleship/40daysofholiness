The Big Picture of God’s Plan for Sanctification

God’s plan for sanctification did not begin when you first desired holiness. Long before creation, God intended to make his people holy. My wife and my father both love puzzles, and at family Christmas a thousand-piece puzzle is often spread across the dining-room table. They do not throw away the box top; they prop it up because its picture shows where every individual piece is headed and gives meaning to the work of putting it together.

Without the box top, scattered puzzle pieces can feel confusing. In the same way, individual teachings, experiences, trials, and blessings make better sense when we see God’s destination for them.

Chosen to Be Holy and Blameless

Ephesians 1:4 says that God chose us in Christ before the creation of the world to be holy and blameless in his sight. Holiness is not a late addition to his plan. Before there was a world, God had already taken the initiative.

Once, while traveling, my family thought we had been invited to supper with a pastor. At the appointed time, we knocked on the door. Because of a miscommunication, the family was shocked to see us; the pastor’s wife even had her hair in curlers. It was an awkward moment that made us wonder whether we had really been invited. Ephesians 1:4 removes that uncertainty about holiness: God invited us. His call is not insincere, and he has prepared for the outcome he commands.

The phrase “in his sight” matters. God does not merely intend a surface appearance that satisfies other people. He intends a holiness and blamelessness that can stand before his own perfect vision.

God Has Never Withheld Holiness

Church backgrounds can sometimes leave people imagining that God teases us with holiness—commanding it while hiding the pieces we need to attain it. That picture is wrong. There has never been a moment in human history when God was not working toward this purpose for his people.

God is more committed to our holiness than we are. The conviction we feel about our hearts is not evidence that he wants to withhold grace; it is part of the way he draws us toward what he planned.

Rest in God’s Plan

Relax into God’s plan to make you like Jesus. Do not fight his drawing or retreat in fear. When he presses upon your heart your need for holiness, receive that conviction with hope. The one who planned your holiness before creation is able to put his plan into practice.

The path ahead is participation in the holiness of God—the very core of his beautiful character. We can pursue it with confidence because the destination was in his heart first.

Guided Prayer

Guided Prayer: Resting in God’s Plan

Father, we praise you for patiently preparing us for this moment. Before time began or the earth was created, you planned to bring holiness into our lives. Draw us into that purpose and take us where you have always intended.

We trust ourselves to your hands. Use every difficulty, trial, pain, negative experience, and blessing in the work of making us holy. Bring to pass the purity and blamelessness you desire in your sight.

Show us the next step. Open our eyes and the doors before us, and we will walk forward in obedience. Remind us that your eternal plan is glorious and that we can rest in your faithful purpose. In Jesus’ name, amen.
