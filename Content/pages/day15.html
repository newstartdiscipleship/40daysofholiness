Day 15 - Possibility Glasses

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

Guided Prayer for Holiness: Possibility Glasses

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Holy Father in Heaven, I rejoice in the possibilities of holiness you have provided for me. Please help me, give me eyes to see and ears to hear, and a heart to rejoice in the possibilities of your infinite grace. I choose today to believe that what you have asked of me, you will provide for me. I choose to see my own weakness through the lens of your grace, and I ask that you fulfill all your promises in my soul. Amen.
