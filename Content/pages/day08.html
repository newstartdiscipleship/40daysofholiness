Day 8 - Holiness has Degrees

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're looking into the book of Leviticus, and asking, "How can things or humans become holy? Once something is holy, can it become MORE holy? How can I grow in holiness?"

We'll examine how God's holiness separates people, places, and things more and more to himself, by looking into the Old Testament and learning what God says about holiness in the book of Leviticus.

Guided Prayer experience: Thanks for joining the 40 Days of Holiness prayer experience. Your workbook contains a written prayer, and the website includes a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be a specific carved-out time of prayer for the Holiness of God in your life. 

Prayer: Father, your holiness is bigger and more expansive than I ever realized. There is more for me, and I am desperate to go there. Only you can take me there. Please do. Plunge me deeper and deeper into the core of your nature, until I shine with the glory of your holiness in more ways than I ever thought I could! Let me find new depths of joy in you, new levels of usefulness for you. Amen
