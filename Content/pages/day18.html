Day 18 - Loving God with All Your Heart

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Father of Holiness, you are worthy of all the love of my heart, soul, mind, and strength! Everything about you is worthy of love and adoration. I want to delight in You, to rejoice in Your will, to desire continually to please You, to seek and find my happiness in You, and to thirst day and night for a fuller enjoyment of You. I long to love you without mixture, without hesitation, without caveat. But I know I will never be able to do it without the work of your Holy Spirit within me, empowering and creating that kind of love. Please enable me to do that, and I will praise and magnify your Name forever!
Amen.
