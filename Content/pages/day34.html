Day 34 - Holiness in Life

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Father in Heaven, I praise you for creating in me the purity of heart and spirit that reflects your beauty and holiness. I pray that your Holy Spirit would also so deeply control and fill my body that I would be enabled to live a holy life in every moment and every situation. I praise you for keeping me in temptation, so that I do not fall into the traps of the Evil One. Give me a patient, holy, disciplined body as I wait for the final redemption you have promised me. When you return, may I be found holy in body and spirit, waiting for your return. Amen.
