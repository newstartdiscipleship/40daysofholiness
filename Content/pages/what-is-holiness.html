What Is Holiness According to the Bible?

What is holiness according to the Bible? Holiness is not an independent quality that a person, place, or object possesses by itself. God alone is intrinsically holy. Everything else becomes holy only through its relationship to him—through his presence, ownership, touch, or use.

I am not an iPhone user, though my wife is—we are a humorously “divided” household. While shopping for her charger, I noticed imitation products made to work with Apple devices even though Apple did not make them. They may eventually break down or work poorly. A mechanic sees the same distinction between aftermarket parts that may not fit and OEM—original equipment manufacturer—parts that do. In the same way, holiness cannot be manufactured apart from God. He is the source, and nothing else can produce the real thing.

Many religions describe a person, place, object, or ceremony as possessing holiness in itself. The biblical pattern is different: when God enters the picture, he communicates his holiness to and through what belongs to him.

Holy Ground and Holy Things

At the burning bush, God told Moses that the ground beneath him was holy (Exodus 3:1–6). There was nothing intrinsically special about that wilderness soil. It was holy because God was there.

Scripture likewise calls bread, oil, pots, sacrifices, the Sabbath, and gold holy when they belong to God or are set apart for his use. Jesus asked, “Which is greater: the gold, or the temple that makes the gold sacred?” (Matthew 23:17). The temple was holy because God chose to place his presence there; its gold was holy through its relationship to the temple and ultimately to God.

The same principle applies to people. God’s presence makes his people holy. First Corinthians 7 even describes an unbelieving spouse and children as sanctified through their relationship to a believer. They have come into contact with a person who is in contact with God.

Holiness Flows Through Connection With God

Holiness is more like flowing electricity than static electricity. Break the circuit and the light goes out. My six sons have used a Snap Circuits board, joining a battery pack, resistors, a siren, and a light into different circuits. Every working circuit must return to its source.

So it is with holiness. It is not produced by gritting our teeth and performing enough “holy” actions. It comes through connection with, filling by, and use by God. The deepest longing for holiness is therefore a longing to be connected to the only source of holiness in the universe.

Guided Prayer

Guided Prayer: Holiness Comes From God

Father, you are beautifully and eternally holy. All holiness begins in you. As your presence made the ground beneath Moses holy, let us encounter you and see the purity, beauty, power, and glory of your holiness.

Everything you touch and possess is holy because you are holy. Flow to us and through us. Produce greater holiness in us until our minds, hearts, and attitudes become like you. Fill even the places within us that we do not yet recognize.

As the bush burned without being consumed, make us shine with your holiness without being destroyed—aglow with its beauty. Show each of us where we most need your holiness to flow today. In Jesus’ name, amen.
