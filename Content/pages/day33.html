Day 33 - The Life of Holiness

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: 

Father, I want my holiness to be as complete as you can make it here on earth. I don’t just want to be rid of the self-sovereign nature, but also of those unconscious, self-centered habits of thought and action that are not like you. Don’t let me get careless and allow old patterns to hang around in my life and grow bitter roots that bear bitter fruits. Please help me to find them, repent and reprogram them – let me become like my Savior, Jesus in those areas. Amen.
