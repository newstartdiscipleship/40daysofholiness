Day 23 - Holiness is Able to be Perfected or Completed

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Father, you are utterly holy, and you have given me your holiness. You have started this project of transformation, you provided it all in Christ! I rejoice in the promises of God, of all that you are and will be for me in holiness. I want to experience the fullness of all those promises – sonship, acceptance, presence. So please help me now as I repudiate self and sinfulness and ask that your purifying touch cleanse out all that is unlike Christ and straighten all that sin has bent in my heart! Amen.
