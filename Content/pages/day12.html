Day 12 - The Twistedness Inside

Welcome to 40 Days of Holiness! Today we're answering the question, "Where does holiness come from? How can things or humans become holy?"

About the Guided Prayer experience: Each day of the 40 Days of Holiness journey will contain not only a written prayer in your Pursuing Holiness workbook, but a 5-minute video prayer time for seeking God's holiness in our hearts. This prayer time is designed to be an anchor, a specific time of seeking the Holiness of God in your life and heart. 

Prayer: Great and Holy Father, thank you for being faithful to me, to root out everything that is not like your son, to straighten out everything that sin has bent. I repent of holding you out of those self-parts of my heart, especially ______, and I ask that you continue to show me all that your Son Jesus has provided to make me fully holy. I am sorry that it's taken me so long to see it and allow you to rule over those things. Thank you that you are rooting out every area of corruption within me. Amen.
