# Transcript-Derived Article Standard

Do not begin bulk transcript processing without explicit owner authorization. Before an article is complete:

- Preserve every substantive part of the teaching. The article is an edited complete teaching, not a summary.
- Smooth grammar, punctuation, repeated starts, and spoken phrasing; remove only empty filler and irrelevant recording housekeeping.
- Do not condense or omit stories, personal illustrations, historical examples, analogies, theology, applications, qualifications, warnings, or memorable phrases. Preserve first-person voice and never replace a detailed story with a short application summary.
- Invent no stories, theology, facts, or experiences. Correct obvious captions carefully; flag uncertain meaning-changing wording rather than guess. Preserve theological and denominational nuance.
- Include Scripture actually read/recited and link each included reference to the appropriate BibleGateway passage. Mentioned-only references may be linked without full text. Distinguish speaker words from quotations.
- Write a natural opening that places the primary SEO topic within roughly 100 words when appropriate. Add useful H2/H3 headings from the teaching’s real organization. Use natural SEO language without stuffing.
- Alt text must accurately describe images; never add keywords merely for SEO. Preserve each original URL and keep the original video prominent near the article.
- Drafts may be `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, never `APPROVED`.

## Fidelity comparison

Inventory every substantive story, illustration, biblical quotation, named person, resource, theological distinction, and application. Verify each appears in the article and spot-check distinctive phrases. Report anything intentionally removed and why. Restore anything removed merely to shorten the page.
